Built the front wheel for Nomu Lab Wheel No. 6

January 16, 2020

Another day with wheels (and so on).

A customer brought me a front wheel built with
a Fillwood hub and SES 4.5 tubular rim.


Black CX-RAY with reverse nipple spoke radial lacing—it's one I built before.
They wanted me to build Nomu Lab Wheel No. 6 using this hub, so


I built it.


20H CX-RAY with reverse nipple spoke radial lacing.


They wanted orange nipples,
but since this is an internal nipple rim they won't be visible, so I used silver nipples instead.

Even though Nomu Lab Wheel No. 6 is discontinued,
I've built it a few times since then,
but those (including this one)
are just built using rims from pre-orders,
and the rim stock is now exhausted.
This front and rear wheel pair is the last stock we have in the shop.

However, if you get a Reynolds 32 wheel or rim
and rebuild the rear wheel using the Nomu Lab method,
you can essentially get a Nomu Lab Wheel No. 6-like wheel...
Many people are thinking about this without me even needing to suggest it, it seems.
