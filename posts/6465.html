Introducing One Crazy Sale!

November 11, 2022

I stocked up on Pearl Izumi (パールイズミ) cloth masks.

The manufacturer ships them in bags with 10 pieces each.
Of course, I'll be selling them individually too.


Stocked up again.


And again and again and again.
Yep, I ordered 200 pieces this time.
It's not a mistake in my order.
The ones crying over this aren't us—
it's the wholesaler or the manufacturer.


These cloth masks are made from the same material as cycling jerseys
and can be washed and reused repeatedly.
The mask has a double-layer construction,
with the middle part forming a pocket
where you can insert tissue and such.


There's only one size available (free size),
with a regular list price of ¥980 (tax-excluded) or ¥1,078 (tax-included),
but this time we're selling them at a one-coin special price of ¥500...







Not a crane...
With one word from our customer-attracting big panda, it came down to ¥200 (tax-included).
Don't blame me—seriously.



I can't go into details on this one.
Personally, it's sketchier than the masks—might get me in trouble.
Even if you call us for details, I can't tell you, so sorry about that.
Let's just call it an in-store customer exclusive deal.
