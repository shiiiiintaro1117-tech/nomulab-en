Built a rear wheel with Grota Tack's 30mm high tubular rim

January 10, 2025

Another wheel day (and so on...).

Built the rear wheel with Grota Tack's 30mm high tubular rim.


DT350 hub, 24H, semi-CX sprint (deliberately not semi-comp)
46 JIS lacing.
I'll do the spoke nipple truing later.



Currently it's a 142mm thru-axle
with XDR freebody, but I'll be changing both.
I couldn't get a 24H rear hub immediately in any other spec,
so I had to source this one reluctantly.

I've been centering the wheel in this configuration, but...

I swapped it out for a Shimano 11-speed freebody
and converted to a 135mm quick-release dropout.



The dimensions came out differently than expected.
The rim was shifted toward the freewheel side—meaning I can tension the non-freewheel side more—
but since the freewheel side was already sufficiently tight,
I eased the freewheel side by half
and tightened the non-freewheel side by half



and re-centered the wheel.
