Built the rear wheel for Nomu Lab Wheel No. 5 with an ONYX Racing hub

July 13, 2021

Back to wheel building today (and so on).

I built the rear wheel for Nomu Lab Wheel No. 5
using an ONYX Racing rear hub.


24H black semi-comp 46-spoke lacing pattern


Same as the front wheel — I arranged the nipples in a clockwise pattern when viewed from the right side,
repeating turquoise, red, gold, and green six times.
I'll do the stringing later.


Like Chris King hubs and similar,
the logo on the hub shell is oriented in the front-to-back direction.
Because of this layout, I couldn't use the rear hub as a reference
to understand the basis for the left-right orientation markings on the front hub
(which in that case were on the hub axle rather than the hub shell).
