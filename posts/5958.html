Built a fixed gear rear wheel with an old Aero 1 rim

November 13, 2021

Wheels again today (and so on).

Continuing from yesterday. Built the rear wheel.
At first glance it looks like the same wheel, but


This is the rear hub.
Since it's a double-threaded hub, I built it JIS style.
Everything else is the same as the front wheel: Shimano Sora Pro large flange 28H
Aero Star Bright #14 with a 6x6 lacing pattern.
I'll do the full cable lacing on both sides later.



The phase of the hub body when viewed through the valve hole—
I set both wheels to the Suntour position,
and with the rim label readable in normal orientation on the right side,


↑Right side

↑Left side
It seems the previous rear wheel that used this hub
had the sprocket mounted on the right side only.


The spokes came with brass nipples with a 3.4mm wrench flat,
but I'm not using those—instead I'm using DT's 3.2mm aluminum nipples.
