Built the rear wheel for Nomu Lab Wheel #5

February 09, 2021

Today it's wheels again (and so on).

I built the rear wheel for Nomu Lab Wheel #5.


Evolite hub, 24 holes, all black, full CX-RAY, 46-cross lacing pattern.



I didn't ask for details since she's a woman
Since the rider is lightweight, I went with full CX-RAY spokes.
I tensioned the freewheel side pretty aggressively and carefully,
but full CX-RAY really does require lacing to true properly.
The freewheel side tension hit a dead end, and I thought I could still tension the non-freewheel side more, so
I put the centering gauge on it and it was already centered.
I'll do the lacing adjustments later.
