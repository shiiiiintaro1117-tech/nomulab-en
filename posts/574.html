Pushing Through

June 27, 2013

I'm currently working through responses to comments and emails about のむラボ (Nomu Lab) wheel orders.
There are some that I haven't been able to reply to for over 10 days.
I'm planning to get back to everyone today.
I apologize for what must have seemed like I was ignoring your messages.

Lately, I've been building wheels after closing time every single day.
During business hours, there's always other work to do, so I can't dedicate all my time to fulfilling のむラボ wheel orders.
The 10+ wheels I've built recently have been almost entirely unrelated to のむラボ wheel orders.
I've only built what's absolutely necessary to clear the backlog before I can even get to the のむラボ wheels, but even non-のむラボ wheels like PowerTap and others still aren't completely finished.
Honestly, I'd love to close the shop for about 10 days and just focus on building wheels.

Even now, I'm building wheels from morning until late at night.
I expect this situation will continue for a while.

If you stop by the shop and I'm stuck at the truing stand, please leave me be.
Internally, I'm giving it everything I've got.


Also, please don't bring gifts.
It really stresses me out. Over the past couple of weeks I've received
Royce chocolate, White Lover biscuits, white ball anmitsu, Iyokan rare cheesecake,
Goroshima gold-sweet potato sablés, Ecletoa chocolate, Flanderoise donuts,
Goncharoff assortment, Henri Charpentier assortment,
kintsuba, Mister Donut, Hakata Tsurimmon, Red Bull, mysterious medicine, and more.
↑If you're thinking "Hey, what I brought isn't on that list!" please point it out (laugh).
I've received even more but my memory is failing me.

And just as I'm writing this, someone brought me coffee beans today.
Complete with a coffee maker.
I'm truly grateful, but I'm also really embarrassed.

In any case, for the time being I want to dedicate every single hour—whether the shop is open or closed—to building wheels.
Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
