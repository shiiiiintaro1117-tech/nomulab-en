Regarding the back pocket zipper pull
on the Oakley (American sportswear brand) discounted windbreaker
from my recent post,
I received a comment suggesting:
"Isn't that a reversible slider
that can flip to either side?"
Since some stock is still available, I decided to investigate.


You were absolutely right!


This is the current state with the pull tucked inside.
The fact that the zipper has this design
suggests it's intended for inside-out storage,


but honestly, this pocket's zipper is quite short,
so it's even difficult to fit, for example,
one of today's larger smartphones in it.
It seems this pocket is really designed
as a dedicated space for storing the jacket itself when turned inside-out.
