Building a suspension wheel with Glowtack 40mm tall tubeless rims

December 13, 2025

I don't have any current plans to make a Nomu Lab Wheel ○ model,
but

in the near future
I'm planning to build front and rear suspension wheels
with Glowtack 40mm tall tubeless rims.


↑The numbers 292240 on the rim
mean outer width 29mm, inner width 22mm, rim height 40mm.

The hub and spokes are the same as Nomu Lab Wheel No. 8,
and I'm planning to build them with the specs that likely
will have the most demand:
black spokes, black nipples, and Shimano HG freebody.
However, some customers considering purchase might say things like
"I would have bought it with silver spokes and red nipples" or
"I would have wanted it with SRAM XDR freebody,"
so I'm allowing a bit of time before building the suspension spec wheels.
If you place an order before I build the suspension spec version,
I'll build it with your preferred specs.
Even if you just want it with black spokes, black nipples, and Shimano,
of course that's absolutely fine too.

The price is 77,000 yen for two rims, and
the Nomu Lab Wheel No. 8 without rims is
73,080 yen for the black spokes + color nipples (the most expensive spec),
so the total comes to 150,080 yen.
Adding rim tape installed at our shop would be
an additional 1,000 yen or so.

Also, for this wheel set only,
I have a coupon code available for
10,000 yen OFF,
and it's written in the usual place (that famous panda shop),
so if you can find your way there,
please definitely use it.

Added December 14th: This has been sold.
Thank you very much
