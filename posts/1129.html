Built Nomu Lab Wheel No. 4

April 28, 2014

Wheels again today (and so on).

I built Nomu Lab Wheel No. 4.
Front 20H and rear 24H.
I'd prefer to build both front and rear as 24H for myself,
but since customer orders for No. 4 tend that way,
when I checked the rim inventory today, the 20H stock was way ahead.

Maybe I'll do the front with No. 4's 20H and the rear with EDGE 1-65's 24H.
Not that it matters since it's just my personal preference.


The front wheel is built with a Leaf hub (リーフハブ) and CX-RAY spokes in reverse radial lacing,


and the rear wheel is built with a Leaf hub in semi-comp four-cross lacing.
