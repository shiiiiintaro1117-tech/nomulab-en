Built Nomu Lab Wheels #1 and #5

February 20, 2017

Another day with wheels (and so on).

Built Nomu Lab Wheel #5.

Front wheel is Evo Lite hub 20H CX-RAY reversed non-drive side radial laced,



Rear wheel is Evo Lite hub 24H semi-comp 46-cross lace with spoke windup.



Built Nomu Lab Wheel #1.

Front wheel is Evo Lite hub 20H CX-RAY reversed non-drive side radial laced,



Rear wheel is Evo Lite hub 24H semi-comp 46-cross lace with spoke windup,


and I went with red aluminum nipples.



I don't usually pay much attention to inventory numbers for the 20H front and 24H rear hubs, but I was supposed to keep roughly equal numbers of each. Turns out the front hubs were 9 short. By the way, these are spares—the Evo Lite hubs I used today were from a different box.

On the right is Leaf hub, top left is Evo Lite hub, bottom left is Black hub. The ones with 2 fitted per slot are front hubs, and the ones with 1 are rear hubs.
