Finally Got Back to Everyone

June 28, 2013

Today another wheel (and so on)—but before that.
I've been meaning to reply to comments and emails that I've left hanging for a while,
I said I'd get back to everyone yesterday,
but it ended up taking until about 9 PM after closing today. I'm really sorry.
I want to improve this slow response time going forward.

Anyway, I'm going to be building Nomu Lab wheels non-stop.
Today I've got 3 front wheels for unit #1. It'll go past midnight, but I'll push a little harder.
The "one day" for the person waiting and the person making them wait is different. I'm in a rush.
Without dropping quality, I'm planning to work through the backlog as quickly as possible.
With the wheels I handed out today, the orders went down by 3,
but the total count has actually gone up to 204 wheels.
I think we'll probably hit a ceiling at around 220 wheels or so.

My immediate goal is to have Nomu Lab wheels on display hanging in the shop.

Alright, time to build some wheels.
